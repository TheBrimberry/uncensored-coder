Tips to maximize yield

  • Chase the rate, not the brand. Online banks change APYs often — check quarterly and move if yours lags by >0.5%.
  • Mind the strings. Some top APYs require direct deposit, a linked checking, or a balance tier. Read the fine print.
  • Money-market funds can beat savings. Brokerage funds (e.g. government MMFs) often track short-term rates closely, but they're not FDIC-insured.
  • Keep an emergency fund liquid. 3–6 months of expenses in a no-penalty, instant-access account before chasing CDs or T-bills.
  • Watch taxes. Interest is taxable as ordinary income; T-bills are state-tax-exempt, which can raise their effective yield.
